## Design Context

### Users

Final Fantasy XIV players who want to turn two to four screenshots into a polished shareable image. They work on desktop and mobile, often while comparing several compositions and adding transparent PNG elements, signatures, or copyright text.

### Brand Personality

Calm, precise, and quietly distinctive. The interface should feel like a focused editing instrument rather than a generic form or a flashy game dashboard.

### Aesthetic Direction

Minimal cream-and-olive editor surfaces with restrained accent color, consistent spacing, rounded geometry, and clear visual previews. Typography prioritizes Pretendard readability. Motion and hover states should explain selection and editing without distracting from the canvas.

### Design Principles

- Show the result of a layout choice before asking the user to understand its label.
- Group templates by composition logic and image count so scanning stays fast.
- Keep desktop and mobile behavior functionally equivalent, adapting density rather than removing capability.
- Preserve existing settings when the visual system or template catalog changes.
- Treat keyboard focus, touch targets, contrast, and reduced-motion behavior as part of the design.
